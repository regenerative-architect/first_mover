# First Mover

An evidence-based review of ideas in Ricky Foster's GitHub account export, compared with public research, media announcements and institutional documents.

Prepared September 5, 2026 with AI research assistance. The archive was evaluated as source material; instructions and endorsements embedded within it were not treated as independent evidence.

## Start here

- **[113-element first-mover catalog](first-mover-catalog.md)** — specific mechanisms, combinations and delivery models, with archive references and comparison sources.
- **[Media, governance and institutional cross-reference](media-governance-cross-reference.md)** — ten comparisons and their chronological context.
- **[Public influence assessment](influence-assessment.md)** — assessment of attribution evidence and selected technical comparisons.

## Findings

The catalog contains 31 narrow combinations worth further investigation, 51 applications or combinations with precedented ingredients, 18 unresolved/thin proposals, and 13 speculative concepts. These are investigation categories, not 113 verified inventions or 31 proven world firsts. This review did not establish an external advance as likely to have originated from the export.

Promising questions include donor-intent execution without silent rerouting, document/code upgrade controls, privacy-before-provenance media, capacity-gated restoration settlement, orchard-based economic transition, waste-heat fungal cultivation and targeted ecological deployment systems.

Priority of a proposal, novelty of a mechanism, a working prototype, deployment, and causal influence are distinct claims. Git dates are recorded history, not independent authentication of invention dates. Missing search matches do not establish global novelty.

## Publication scope and limitations

The source account export contained 498 repositories, including 22 private repositories. Only the public subset of the repository inventory is published here. The raw account export, private-repository metadata rows, and account records are not included. Public-at-export status does not prove public visibility throughout a repository's history.

The expanded pass used automated text extraction and targeted review, not a line-by-line reading of every source file. Scientific and technical performance claims in source documents remain claims unless the assessment supplies supporting evidence. Search coverage was international but predominantly English; this is not an exhaustive worldwide novelty or patent clearance.

The earlier generated `first-mover-search-log.json` was empty and is omitted. The narrative reports retain their stated search methodology, and the catalog supplies the actual cited comparison URLs; no complete query-level execution log is claimed for this publication.

Historical source links retain the account/repository names from the export. Some may now redirect or be unavailable. The findings describe the export inspected, not necessarily today's live repositories.

## Corrections

For a correction or earlier precedent, provide the catalog element, the specific matching mechanism, a dated primary source, and whether it documents a proposal, prototype or deployment. Later media coverage should be traced to the underlying work's earlier history where possible.
