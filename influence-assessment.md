## What would justify “likely stemmed from it”

A persuasive connection needs more than matching subject matter and a later headline. Strong evidence would include a citation or acknowledgement, a downstream fork or distinctive code reuse, correspondence connecting a team to a specific version, or an unusually specific technical match coupled with evidence of access and the correct chronology.

Repository creation and commit timestamps help locate documents in time. They do not, by themselves, establish when another organization read a document, whether a claimed system worked, or whether its author originated every underlying idea. An authenticated timestamp would strengthen dating, but would still not establish adoption. The blockchain/notarization claims in the archive were not independently verified in this review.
